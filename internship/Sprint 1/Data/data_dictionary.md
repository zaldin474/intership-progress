| Column                   | Type                 | Description                                                                                |
| ------------------------ | -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Age**                  | Numerical            | Age of the patient in years.                                                               |
| **Gender**               | Categorical          | Gender of the patient.                                                                     |
| **Blood Pressure**       | Numerical            | Blood pressure measurement of the patient.                                                 |
| **Cholesterol Level**    | Numerical            | Total cholesterol level of the patient.                                                    |
| **Exercise Habits**      | Categorical          | Indicates the patient's level or frequency of physical activity.                           |
| **Smoking**              | Categorical          | Indicates whether the patient smokes.                                                      |
| **Family Heart Disease** | Categorical          | Indicates whether there is a family history of heart disease.                              |
| **Diabetes**             | Categorical          | Indicates whether the patient has diabetes.                                                |
| **BMI**                  | Numerical            | Body Mass Index, representing weight relative to height.                                   |
| **High Blood Pressure**  | Categorical          | Indicates whether the patient has been identified as having high blood pressure.           |
| **Low HDL Cholesterol**  | Categorical          | Indicates whether the patient has low HDL ("good") cholesterol.                            |
| **High LDL Cholesterol** | Categorical          | Indicates whether the patient has high LDL ("bad") cholesterol.                            |
| **Alcohol Consumption**  | Categorical          | Describes the patient's alcohol consumption status or level.                               |
| **Stress Level**         | Categorical          | Represents the patient's reported stress level.                                            |
| **Sleep Hours**          | Numerical            | Average number of hours the patient sleeps.                                                |
| **Sugar Consumption**    | Categorical          | Represents the patient's level of sugar consumption.                                       |
| **Triglyceride Level**   | Numerical            | Blood triglyceride level of the patient.                                                   |
| **Fasting Blood Sugar**  | Numerical            | Blood glucose level measured after fasting.                                                |
| **CRP Level**            | Numerical            | C-reactive protein level, an indicator associated with inflammation.                       |
| **Homocysteine Level**   | Numerical            | Homocysteine concentration in the blood, which may be associated with cardiovascular risk. |
| **Heart Disease Status** | Target / Categorical | Target variable indicating whether the patient has heart disease.                          |
